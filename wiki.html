Start here

Watch Later Enhanced is a private watch list for YouTube. You save videos with one click while you browse, and they wait for you in the extension popup — no playlists, no clutter, no account.

1

Save

Hold Alt and click any video on YouTube. A short confirmation appears on screen.

2

Open the popup

Click the extension icon in the browser toolbar to see everything you saved.

3

Watch

Click a title to open the video, or send it to the floating mini player.

Can't find the extension icon? Click the puzzle-piece button in the browser toolbar and pin Watch Later Enhanced so it stays visible.

Saving a video

There are three ways to add a video to your list. All of them work the same way and save the same thing.

1. Alt + Click on a thumbnail

Anywhere on YouTube — home page, search results, a channel, the sidebar of recommendations — hold Alt and click a video's thumbnail or title. The video is saved and YouTube does not navigate away, so you can keep browsing and keep saving.

2. Alt + Click on the player

While you are watching something, hold Alt and click the video player itself to save the video you are on.

3. The button under the video

On a video page, Watch Later Enhanced adds a Save to Watch Later Enhanced button next to YouTube's own Like and Share buttons.

What you get back

  • A dark confirmation panel appears in the middle of the page with the video title.
  • A short sound plays (you can turn this off in Settings).
  • If the video is already on your list, the panel says Already saved! instead of adding a duplicate.
Shorts work too. Regular videos and Shorts are both saved, and both end up as a normal watch link in your list — so a Short opens as an ordinary video when you come back to it. Its title is read from the Shorts player itself, because on those pages the browser tab is called just "YouTube".

Your list

The popup has three tabs. The number on each tab tells you how many videos are inside.

To Watch

Everything you saved and haven't watched yet. This is the tab you will use most.

Archive

Videos you marked as watched. They leave To Watch but are not deleted.

Trash

Removed videos, kept for a while in case you change your mind.

What each row tells you

Under every title sits a small label — Video or Shorts — and next to it the channel that published it. A long channel name is shortened; hover it to read the whole thing. Your own tags go on their own line underneath, so the two never crowd each other.

Under the search box, three chips narrow the list to All, Videos or Shorts, each with a count of how many there are. The choice sticks until you change it, and works alongside the tag search.

The type is worked out from the page a video was saved from, and then confirmed with YouTube in the background — because the same Short can be opened either as a Short or as an ordinary video, and from a /watch link nothing on the page says which it is. A label that starts out wrong corrects itself a moment later.

Items you saved before a detail existed — a type, a channel — are not left blank. A few of them are filled in each time you open a YouTube page, quietly in the background. A long list takes a while to work through, and needs nothing from you.

Undo

After you archive or remove a video, a small bar appears at the bottom of the popup with an Undo link for a couple of seconds. It puts the video back exactly where it was.

How the Trash behaves

  • The Trash keeps your 50 most recent removals. Older ones drop off on their own to keep storage small.
  • Empty trash, at the top of the Trash tab, clears it in one go.
  • Emptying the Trash and deleting permanently cannot be undone.

Tags & search

Tags are free-form labels — cooking, work, watch tonight, anything you like. A video can have as many as you want.

Adding a tag

  1. Click the tag button on the video's row.
  2. Type a name (up to 22 characters). Tags you already use are suggested as you type.
  3. Press Enter to add it. Press Esc to cancel.

Each tag gets its own colour automatically, derived from its name — the same word always gets the same colour.

Finding videos again

  • Click a tag on any row to show only the videos carrying exactly that tag.
  • Type in the search box at the top to filter by tag name as you type — partial words are fine.
  • Clear the search box to see the whole list again.

Removing a tag

Click the small × inside the tag itself. It only affects that one video.

The search box looks at tags, not video titles. Tag the things you will want to find later.

Reordering your list

In the To Watch tab, grab the handle on the left of a row and drag it up or down to set your own order. Drag near the top or bottom edge and the list scrolls by itself.

Dragging is switched off while a tag search is active — otherwise the positions you see would not match the real list. Clear the search box and the handles come back.

Detached mini player

The mini player pops the video out of the browser into a small floating window that stays on top of your other applications. You can drag it anywhere on screen, resize it, and keep watching while the browser is minimized or you work in another program.

Three ways to open it

  • Click the pop-out button (a square with an arrow leaving it) in YouTube's own control bar, at the bottom right of the video. Careful not to confuse it with YouTube's own miniplayer button a few icons away, which only shrinks the video inside the tab.
  • Press Alt + Shift + D on a video page.
  • Click the mini player button on any row in the extension popup — the screen with a play symbol. The video starts playing right away in a small window of its own — no tab, nothing to confirm.

Pinning it on top

A mini player opened from a video page — the control-bar button or Alt + Shift + D — already floats above every other application. The window opened from the popup does not: it survives minimizing the browser, but other windows can cover it.

To fix that, click Pin on top in the top-right corner of that window. The video moves into a floating player that stays above everything else, and the window it came from tells you where it went. Close the pinned player and the video comes straight back.

Pinning is a button rather than a setting on purpose: browsers only hand out an always-on-top window in answer to a click on the page itself, so nothing can ask for one on your behalf.

While it is open

  • YouTube's real controls come along, so play, volume, captions, quality, chapters and the skip-ad button all keep working.
  • The YouTube tab shows a placeholder with a Bring the video back button where the player used to be.
  • The window remembers the size you gave it and opens that way next time.

Closing it

Any of these puts the video back into the YouTube tab, where it keeps playing:

  • The × in the top-right corner of the mini player. It stays visible even when the bar fades, and turns red as you point at it.
  • The button beside it — the same window with the arrow coming back in — which also brings the YouTube tab to the front.
  • Esc, the same button in YouTube's control bar, or Alt + Shift + D again.
  • Navigating to another page in the YouTube tab — the video returns automatically.

What your browser needs

  • Chrome, Edge and other Chromium browsers, version 116 or newer: the full floating window described above.
  • Browsers without it: the extension falls back to the classic picture-in-picture window, which also floats on top but carries only basic controls.
  • Neither available: a short message appears on the page and nothing else changes.
You can switch the whole feature off under Settings → Detached mini player. That hides the button in YouTube's control bar, the button in the popup and disables the shortcut.

Settings

Open the popup and click the gear icon in the top-right corner. Sound, Mini player and JobsMatch banner are folded away until you click them, and each closed header says where that setting currently stands. The user guide and the supporter code sit outside, always in view.

Sound

All sounds is the master switch: off means silence, wherever the sound would have come from.

Below it is a mixer with one row per sound. Click is what you hear in the popup; Save is what plays on YouTube when a video is added. Each has its own switch and its own level — drag the slider, or type a number from 0 to 100 in the box beside it. Muting a sound keeps the level you gave it, so switching it back on sounds the way it did.

Detached mini player

Turns the floating mini player on and off, everywhere. It is on by default.

User guide

The book button that opened this page. It works offline — the guide ships inside the extension.

Supporter code and the JobsMatch note

The extension is free. A small note about JobsMatch sits under the header to support the project. After a tip on Ko-fi you receive a code; pasting it in Settings unlocks a switch that hides the note permanently.

Privacy & your data

  • Your list lives in your own browser, in the extension's local storage. There is no account, no sign-in and no server holding your data.
  • Nothing you save is sent anywhere. The only request the extension makes is to YouTube itself, to read the title of a video as you save it.
  • The extension only runs on YouTube pages. It cannot see any other site you visit.
  • Because the list is stored in this browser profile, it does not follow you to another computer — and removing the extension removes the list with it.
Moving to a new machine? There is no export button yet, so treat the list as tied to this browser.

Troubleshooting

Alt + Click opened the video instead of saving it

The click has to land on the video's thumbnail or its title. Hold Alt first, then click, and keep the key down until the confirmation appears. On some Linux desktops Alt + drag is reserved by the system for moving windows — a plain click still works.

The Save button isn't under the video

YouTube sometimes finishes drawing its buttons late. Reload the page. If the button is still missing, the video page may be an unusual layout — Alt + click on the player always works.

The mini player button isn't in the control bar

  • Check Settings → Detached mini player is on.
  • Reload the video page.
  • Very old browsers have no support for floating windows at all — see the requirements above.

I clicked the mini player button in the popup and it only opened the video

That is the first half of the flow. Look at the middle of the video page: a card titled Open the mini player is waiting for a click. A browser only opens a floating window immediately after a click inside that page, so the popup cannot do it on its own. If you dismissed the card, use the pop-out button in the player's control bar or Alt + Shift + D.

I got a small player inside the page instead of a floating window

That is YouTube's own miniplayer, which lives in the corner of the tab and disappears when you minimize the browser. Its button sits in the same control bar as ours. The Watch Later Enhanced button is the square with an arrow leaving it.

The mini player closed by itself

That is expected when the YouTube tab navigates somewhere else: the video is handed back to the tab so nothing is lost. Reopen it on the new page.

"Could not save — storage may be full"

The browser refused the write because the extension's storage is full. Empty the Trash and permanently delete what you no longer need.

A video shows the wrong title

Titles are read from YouTube when you save. If the request fails, the extension falls back to the text next to the thumbnail, which is occasionally truncated. Remove the video and save it again from the video's own page for the exact title.
